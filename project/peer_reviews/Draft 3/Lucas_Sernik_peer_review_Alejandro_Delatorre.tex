\textbf{{\Large Peer review of Alejandro's Paper}}

\vspace{1cm}

The draft provides a great introduction to the paper, which will be about the effects of a less burocratic process for approving new drugs in the drug market in general, with a focus on the impacts of the opioids and general population health. More controlled substances available to the general public might be associated with higher overdose and some kind of worsening of public health conditions. Even though the introduction is well done and has a lot of information, I believe that it has too much information. I recommend that the author decreases the size of the introduction, keeping only more relevant background information for the results that he eventually produces
